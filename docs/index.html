Credit Risk Scoring Lab

A closed-loop MLOps platform for credit risk, not a notebook of models: eleven from-scratch statistical techniques for the scoring question itself, wired into fifteen more that detect drift, retrain, deploy, canary-test, cut over, monitor realized performance, and audit the whole system — honestly, including the state where retraining fired and nobody closed the loop yet.

27 · On a real book: Lending Club, 621,022 loans

Techniques 01-11 run on simulated books because their claims are recoveries of a planted truth. Folder 27 takes the claims that are not, and runs them through the same code on real Lending Club loans issued 2007-2015, fitted on 2007-2013 and tested on 2015. The monotonic constraint is still free and conformal coverage holds a year later; the scorecard no longer beats gradient boosting, the binning methods tie, 72% of lifetime risk arrives after month 12 (50.5% simulated), a rating grade's asset correlation is 0.00-0.06 rather than 0.12-0.24, and the retraining rule fires on AUC every quarter while missing a 2.5-point PD understatement.
